1. Customer Cohort Analysis
Business Question: After how many months do customers typically cancel their subscriptions?

Insight:

Cohort heatmap shows a steady diagonal decline in active customers across months.

Most cohorts experience a sharp drop between Month 3 – 5, indicating that customers typically cancel within 3 – 5 months of starting their subscription.

Later cohorts (especially December 2025) have larger starting sizes, suggesting growth in acquisition, but the same churn pattern persists.

Conclusion:

Customers generally cancel after 3–5 months, highlighting the need for retention strategies during this period.

 2. Revenue Forecast
Business Question: What is the expected subscription revenue over the next 12 months? Does the forecast show evidence of seasonality?

Insight:

Revenue shows a consistent upward trend from early 2024 through 2026.

Forecast predicts continued growth into 2027, reaching approximately $320K–$400K monthly revenue.

Minor dips around December each year suggest seasonal fluctuations, likely due to holiday cycles or renewal timing.

Conclusion:

Revenue is expected to grow steadily over the next 12 months with mild seasonality around year‑end months.

 3. CAC vs LTV Analysis
Business Question: How long does it take for the 2024 and 2025 cohorts to recover their customer acquisition cost (CAC)?

Insight:

The Total CAC reference line is constant at ≈ 1.66 M.

The cumulative LTV curve surpasses the CAC line around September 2025, meaning the cohorts become profitable after roughly 9 months.

Post‑recovery, LTV continues to rise sharply, confirming positive ROI and sustainable growth.

Conclusion:

The 2024 and 2025 cohorts recover CAC within 9 months, after which customer lifetime value exceeds acquisition cost.
